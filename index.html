A working preview of the Lapnex store for design review. The products, prices, orders and policy text are samples. Open it on your phone and on a computer.

Start at the home page How to try it

How to try it

  1. 1

    Find a laptop. On the listing page, use the price slider first, then the other filters. The counts and results update as you go.

  2. 2

    Customise it. On a product page, choose a RAM or storage upgrade and watch the price change.

  3. 3

    Add to cart and check out. The cart has 3 sample laptops to start. Try each payment method; on the card option you can pretend the payment fails.

  4. 4

    Follow the order. After you place an order, upload a receipt, then track it or find it under My orders.

This preview needs an internet connection (it loads fonts and scripts online). Orders and the cart are saved in your own browser only, so nothing is sent anywhere. To start over, clear this site's data in your browser.
